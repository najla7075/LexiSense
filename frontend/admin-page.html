What You Can Do in Class Tomorrow (Teacher Action Guide)

Quick, proven ways to make reading comfortable for your learners right away

📏 1. Use Reading Rulers

Place a colored ruler or bookmark under each line. Stops kids from skipping lines and losing their place.

⏱️ 2. Extra 5–10 Minutes

Give slightly more time during reading exercises. Takes away test anxiety and helps sound out tricky words.

🔤 3. Clear, Bigger Fonts

Print worksheets using friendly fonts like Lexend at 14–16pt. Makes letters like b and d easy to tell apart.
