Important Clinical & Medical Notice

LexiSense is an observational artificial intelligence screening tool. It is designed to assist parents and educators in detecting early risk markers (such as saccadic regressions, phoneme decoding speed, and letter flipping). LexiSense is NOT a substitute for a comprehensive neuropsychological or medical diagnosis conducted by a registered clinical psychologist or speech-language therapist.
