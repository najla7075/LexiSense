Learning Difference

What is Dyslexia? 🧠

Dyslexia is simply a neurodevelopmental learning difference that affects how the brain connects written letters to spoken sounds.

💡 Superpower Fact for Parents:

Dyslexia has NOTHING to do with intelligence or eyesight! Dyslexic thinkers are gifted with incredible 3D spatial reasoning, creative storytelling, and big-picture problem solving!

Dispelling Dyslexia Myths

Discover the scientific facts behind reading differences:

Myth #1 Vision

"Dyslexia is just seeing letters backwards or upside down."

✓ Scientific Fact: Dyslexia is phonological, not eyesight-related. Reversing letters (b/d) is normal for young kids; dyslexic minds struggle matching speech sounds to symbols.
Myth #2 Growth

"Children will outgrow dyslexia automatically as they get older."

✓ Scientific Fact: Dyslexia is lifelong, but early multisensory phonics before age 8 rewires neural pathways, creating confident readers!
Myth #3 IQ

"Dyslexic children are less intelligent or just lazy."

✓ Scientific Fact: Dyslexia has ZERO link to intelligence. Dyslexic kids expend 5x more mental energy decoding words while possessing gifted creative minds.
Myth #4 Practice

"Just forcing them to read more books will fix dyslexia."

✓ Scientific Fact: Forcing reading without decoding tools causes anxiety. Line rulers, high contrast, and structured phonics work best!
