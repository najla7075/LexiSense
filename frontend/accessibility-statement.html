Formal WCAG Compliance & Technical Standards

WCAG 2.1 Level AA Verified

1. Accessibility Mandate

LexiSense AI Inc. is dedicated to ensuring digital equality for all users, including those with learning differences, sensory sensitivities, and motor challenges. We continually enhance user experience by adopting modern accessibility standards and collaborating with special education technologists.

2. Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. LexiSense is fully conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA, with critical components such as typography contrast, focus rings, and non-reliance on color meeting Level AAA benchmarks.

3. Keyboard Navigation & Assistive Compatibility

All interactive modules, including screening quest choices, modal dialogs, and parent portal charts, are fully operable via standard keyboard controls (Tab, Shift + Tab, Enter, Space, and Escape). All interactive elements provide visible focus outlines and descriptive ARIA landmarks.

4. Continuous Co-Design & Feedback Channel

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of LexiSense. If you encounter accessibility obstacles, please let us know:

Accessibility Lead: accessibility@lexisense.ai

Response Time: We endeavor to respond to accessibility inquiries within 2 business days.
